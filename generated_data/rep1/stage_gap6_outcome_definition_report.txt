PART 2 — BASELINE ELIGIBILITY, AND WHY THE COHORT'S EVENT PROPORTION IS NOT AN INCIDENCE
====================================================================================================

  MIMIC-IV patients with any diagnosis row          :   180640
  ... with a CKD stage 3-5 code (eligible)          :    10179 (5.63% of the above)
  ... with an `esrd_codes` code                     :    31542
  ... with BOTH (label-positive population)         :     7389 (72.59% of eligible patients)

  The extracted scenarios do NOT sample this population proportionally. Each rep
  is built by drawing label-positive and label-negative patients separately
  (time_series_store.process_esrd_patients / process_negative_patients, joined in
  model_data_store), so the 83-92% label-positive proportion reported for the
  extracted cohorts is a DESIGN PARAMETER of that draw plus the lab-availability
  filtering that follows it — not an observed incidence and not a risk.

  RETRACTION (Gap 6): the earlier claim that this cohort's incidence is 'one to
  two orders of magnitude lower' than KFRE's validation populations should not be
  repeated. It compared a sampled label proportion against a fixed-horizon risk,
  which are not the same kind of quantity in either direction. The correct
  statement is that the two are not commensurable, and why.

  For the record, KFRE's own populations, so the difference can be described
  rather than quantified against an incommensurable number:
    - Tangri et al. 2011, JAMA 305(15):1553-1559 (PMID 21482743): two
      nephrology-REFERRED CKD stage 3-5 cohorts in Ontario, development and
      validation, with kidney-failure event proportions of 11% and 24%.
    - Tangri et al. 2016, JAMA 315(2):164-174 (PMID 26757465): multinational
      validation across 31 cohorts, extending beyond the referred setting.
    - Endpoint in both: need for maintenance dialysis or preemptive kidney
      transplantation. Horizons: 2 and 5 years.

PART 3 — PREDICTION HORIZON SUPPORT IN THE EXTRACTED COHORTS
====================================================================================================

  KFRE is applied at its published 2-year and 5-year horizons without refitting,
  while the learned models are fitted to this cohort. Whether those horizons are
  supported here is a property of the extracted follow-up:

    four_features: 450 held-out patients, 376 label-positive (83.6%)
      2-year  (730 d): 281 events inside the window, 33 censored before it, 136 followed to or past it
      5-year  (1825 d): 342 events inside the window, 52 censored before it, 56 followed to or past it
      follow-up: median 37 d, p90 2053 d, max 3942 d

    eight_features: 194 held-out patients, 165 label-positive (85.1%)
      2-year  (730 d): 147 events inside the window, 22 censored before it, 25 followed to or past it
      5-year  (1825 d): 155 events inside the window, 26 censored before it, 13 followed to or past it
      follow-up: median 0 d, p90 1110 d, max 3843 d

    twenty_features_heterogeneous: 5216 held-out patients, 4770 label-positive (91.4%)
      2-year  (730 d): 3602 events inside the window, 285 censored before it, 1329 followed to or past it
      5-year  (1825 d): 4235 events inside the window, 355 censored before it, 626 followed to or past it
      follow-up: median 125 d, p90 2065 d, max 5049 d

  Reading: where most label-positive patients reach the event well inside two
  years, a 2-year KFRE risk and a model score fitted on this cohort are being
  asked different questions of different populations, and a head-to-head
  discrimination difference between them reflects that as much as it reflects
  model quality. Any superiority claim should be scoped to this cohort and this
  protocol.

====================================================================================================
PART 4 — WHAT THE MANUSCRIPT SHOULD SAY
====================================================================================================

  1. Name the outcome for what it is: an acute/unspecified kidney-failure
     diagnosis code recorded in a patient with CKD stage 3-5, dated to the
     admission that carried it. Do not call it ESRD without that qualification,
     and do not describe it as the KFRE endpoint.
  2. State that KFRE's endpoint is maintenance dialysis or preemptive
     transplantation, that this differs from the labelled outcome here, and that
     an equation applied to a different endpoint should not be expected to
     calibrate — so a calibration gap is partly definitional, not only a
     population effect.
  3. Remove the 'one to two orders of magnitude lower' incidence comparison; say
     instead that the extraction draws label-positive and label-negative patients
     separately, so its label proportion is not an incidence and is not
     comparable to KFRE's cohort risks.
  4. State that the learned models are fitted to this cohort while KFRE is
     applied without refitting, so the comparison is between a fitted and an
     externally-specified model. Limit superiority claims to this cohort and
     protocol.
  5. Coordinate with Gap 8: the input cutoff coincides with the event date for
     label-positive patients, which bears on whether any score here can be read
     as prospective risk.

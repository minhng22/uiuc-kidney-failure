Why same-admission uACR was loosened
--------------------------------------
Same-admission uACR bound (first pilot pass, PID 1027916/1027933) caused
96.5% patient attrition (34,332 → 1,220 for four_features), disproportion-
ately dropping ESRD-negative patients (0.8% retained vs. 3.8% for
ESRD-positive), skewing the cohort's outcome rate from the source
population's 91.9% positive to 98.2% positive. Per user direction, loosened
to whole-patient-history matching (see EXPERIMENT_PLAN_DETAILS.md "1a-2"
addendum for the full citation-backed rationale) and reran clean (PID
1032596).

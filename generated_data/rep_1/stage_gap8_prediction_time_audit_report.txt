PART A — PREDICTION TARGET PER MODEL (read from pkgs/models/*.py's predictions())
====================================================================================================

Shared across all 11 models:
  Time origin   : days since that patient's FIRST lab record in the exported frame
                  (time_series_utils_store.calculate_duration_in_days). The origin is
                  NOT reset at the landmark, so a reported 730-day horizon is measured
                  from the first record, not from the row the score is attached to.
  Outcome       : has_esrd, from ICD diagnosis codes (pkgs/commons.esrd_codes).
                  ESRD-positive patients' rows are truncated at the first ESRD
                  diagnosis DATE and only rows on that date carry has_esrd=1
                  (time_series_store.process_patient_esrd); ESRD-negative patients
                  are censored at their last lab record.
  Landmark      : every model is scored on one row/curve per patient. Nine models
                  read the patient's LAST exported row; Dynamic-DeepHit and Hazard
                  Transformer pool the whole sequence up to that row.

  CONSEQUENCE, stated plainly: for an ESRD-positive patient the landmark row is the
  last row at or before the diagnosis date, so the input cutoff is the outcome date,
  not a decision time some fixed interval before it. The duration attached to that
  patient is time from first record to diagnosis, which is a follow-up length, not a
  prediction lead time. Part B quantifies how large that distinction is.

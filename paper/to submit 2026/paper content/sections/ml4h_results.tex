\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

All 160 model--scenario--run metric records are available across five runs: 11 models each for four/eight features and 10 for twenty features. Tables~\ref{tab:discrimination} and~\ref{tab:twenty} report mean (sample SD) on the fixed patient partition. Rep99 pilot results are excluded; SD measures run variation, not uncertainty across independent test cohorts.

Dynamic-DeepHit has the highest recorded C-index, 0.827 (0.011) and 0.880 (0.009), and mean AUC, 0.939 (0.017) and 0.953 (0.009), for four and eight features. KFRE has C-index 0.539 in both scenarios. Hazard Transformer follows at 0.637 (0.001) and 0.605 (0.039). The remaining non-Cox models cluster at 0.518--0.552 and 0.484--0.554. These descriptive comparisons do not establish equivalence or statistical superiority.

Four-to-eight-feature changes are model-dependent: Dynamic-DeepHit +0.053, Cox +0.162, DeepSurv -0.056, and Hazard Transformer -0.032 in mean C-index; the other seven models change by 0.03 or less. The cohorts differ, so this is not an isolated test of adding four measurements. Cox remains below 0.5 (0.308/0.470); DeepSurv's eight-feature mean is also below 0.5.

The full cohort contains 32{,}601 patients and approximately 8.13 million lab-event rows. Dynamic-DeepHit leads at C-index 0.856 (0.035), mean AUC 0.946 (0.026), and transformed-score IBS 0.248 (0.013). Hazard Transformer reaches C-index 0.717 (0.060), replacing the earlier poor pilot result. The other non-Cox models span 0.513--0.533; Cox is 0.457. Cohort and representation differences prevent attribution to feature count alone.

\subsection{Importance and Calibration Diagnostics}
Figure~\ref{fig:feature_importance_four} illustrates rep1 importance. eGFR ranks first for four neural models, whereas GBSA emphasizes uACR and eGFR similarly. Raw coefficients, permutation scores, and gradients use different scales; a uACR coefficient rounded to 0.0000 does not imply that the input was discarded. All five twenty-feature DDH/Transformer gradient analyses failed memory allocation; their fallback values are excluded from interpretation.

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/four_features_all_models_feature_importance.png}
\caption{Four-feature importance diagnostics, rep1. Model-specific measures are not comparable across panels.}
\label{fig:feature_importance_four}
\end{figure}

Native risk calibration varies across runs. For twenty-feature rep1, Dynamic-DeepHit's 2-year risk-group means are 0.166--0.187 versus observed risks of 0.431--0.998, despite strong discrimination. Hazard Transformer also produces constant or near-constant horizon probabilities. The reported IBS uses transformed scalar scores and does not evaluate these native probabilities directly.

Decision-curve comparisons are withheld: model curves use patients, whereas treat-all/eGFR baselines use raw rows; the eGFR rule also uses a different threshold definition. The saved curves therefore do not establish clinical utility.
